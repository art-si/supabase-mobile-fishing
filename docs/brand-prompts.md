# 钓友集图像生成提示词

工具：内置 image_gen；头像以横版 Logo 为图像参考。

## 横版 Logo

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final primary horizontal logo PNG for a Chinese fishing community app and WeChat mini program named 钓友集.
Primary request: design one original, beautifully simple, scalable identity that combines a fish with the friendly shape of a conversation bubble, suggesting anglers sharing catches and finding fishing friends.
Style/medium: precise flat vector-like graphic, strong silhouette, bold smooth economical curves, calm outdoors character, refined contemporary Chinese mobile brand. The fish mark must feel natural and distinctive, not a cartoon mascot. Use one small floating bobber vermilion accent only if it helps the silhouette.
Color palette from the existing app: lake teal #0F766E for the fish mark, deep green ink #182E29 for the wordmark, tiny muted vermilion #9C512F accent. Opaque ink, genuinely transparent surrounding background.
Text (verbatim): "钓友集" — exactly these three Simplified Chinese characters, correctly formed, highly legible, medium-bold contemporary sans serif with gently rounded human warmth. No other text.
Composition/framing: a single horizontal lockup, fish symbol on the left and three-character wordmark on the right, carefully optically aligned, comfortable gap, balanced clear space. Wide landscape approximately 3:1 canvas. Make the symbol and wordmark large within the canvas with practical modest transparent padding.
Constraints: final usable logo asset, actual alpha transparency, crisp smooth edges, no background color, no checkerboard baked into image, no presentation sheet, no extra logo options, no slogan, no English, no mockup, no scenery, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no texture, no watermark. The compact symbol must remain recognizable as both a fish and a conversation mark at small icon sizes.
```

## 小程序头像

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production square avatar icon for the WeChat mini program 钓友集, also usable as a mobile app icon.
Input images: Image 1 is the identity source. Preserve the source fish symbol's exact recognizable silhouette: fish facing right, speech bubble tail at lower left, three conversation dots in the body, the arched fishing line and small split float above its mouth. The Chinese wordmark in the source is omitted in this icon.
Primary request: create the matching icon variant of this identity. Change the fish symbol and the arched line to solid warm white #F5F7F3, keep the bobber accent muted orange-vermilion #D98860 for visibility. All fish cutouts including the eye and the three conversation dots show the teal background through negative space. Refine all shapes to clean flat, smooth opaque fills; retain the source symbol's proportions and personality. No text anywhere.
Background: edge-to-edge perfectly solid lake teal #0F766E, fully opaque. Square 1:1 canvas, preferably 1024x1024 or larger. No transparency.
Composition: one centered fish symbol approximately 70% of canvas width, balanced vertical optical centering with the arched line. Keep every part safely inside an invisible central circle 82% of canvas width so WeChat's round avatar crop preserves the entire mark. Comfortable uniform margins. Full square background, no baked-in rounded corners.
Constraints: final standalone upload-ready icon, a single icon only. Crisp minimal flat artwork, no gradient, no texture, no shadows, no bevel, no 3D, no border, no scenery, no mockup, no lettering, no watermark. Maintain identity fidelity to Image 1.
```
